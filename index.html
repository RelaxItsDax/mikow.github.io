About

Who am I?

Photo

I'm Dax. My three favorite games are The Finals, Path of Exile, and Outer Wilds.

I started making games when I was 11 with Scratch. When I was 15, I joined a community game design team for Hypixel Skyblock. One of my friends there taught me SpigotMC and Java, and I started making Minecraft servers. Then, wanting to make "real" games, I picked up Unity. The rest is history.

As I went through High School and College, my love for game development only grew. I joined the UMBC Game Developers Club, eventually becoming President. I've worked on countless games and met many friends through it. My dream is to one day start my own studio!
